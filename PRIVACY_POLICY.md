# Privacy Policy for HabitFlow

**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

HabitFlow ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Dhanya Kannan as a free mobile application. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**HabitFlow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information.**

- **Local Storage:** All your data (such as habit names, descriptions, schedules, logs, and streak history) is stored locally on your device using an offline SQLite database. We do not have access to your personal data or habit history.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address or password to use HabitFlow.
- **No External Servers:** HabitFlow operates completely offline. No user data is sent to external servers or cloud services.

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

HabitFlow requests the following device permissions only to support core app functionality:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to deliver local reminder notifications for your scheduled habits at the times you select.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Used to ensure habit reminders fire precisely at the requested times and reschedule alarms after your device restarts.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`):** Used to provide haptic feedback and vibrate the device when a reminder notification arrives.

None of these permissions are used to collect or export your personal data.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

HabitFlow does **not** integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking services, or marketing SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device:
- You have full control over your data.
- You can delete individual habits or reset your data anytime within the App settings.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing app storage will permanently remove all stored habits and history.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

HabitFlow does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, as no personal data is collected from any user.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
